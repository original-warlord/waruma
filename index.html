Let us take a moment to evaluate the scenario:




RED FLAGS:


1. UNEXPECTED VEHICLE TO PROPERTY


A. NO BUSINESS MARKINGS


2. SUBJECT DIDN'T IDENTIFY THEMSELVES ON CONTACT


A. NO BUSINESS ATTIRE OR LOGO ON CLOTHING


B. NO VISIBLE ID BADGE WITH COMPANY LOGO


C. UNPROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR (ASKING CUSTOMER TO RETRIEVE OWN SUPPOSED DELIVERY


D. RELUCTANCE TO IDENTIFY NAME OF COMPANY





IMPROVEMENTS TO REACTIONS:




1. SITUATIONAL AWARENESS ON INITIAL CONTACT




2. DIDN'T TAKE PHOTOGRAPH OF SUBJECT ON CONTACT




3. DIDN'T IMMEDIATELY CALL 9-1-1 ON OBSERVING NO LOGOS ON VEHICLE OR WITH SUSPICIOUS BEHAVIOR OF THE SUBJECT (RELUCTANCE TO IDENTIFY BUSINESS AND ASKING FOR HELP DOING JOB)





POSITIVE REACTIONS DURING SCENARIO:


1. STAYED ON OTHER SIDE OF BARRIER (GATE)


2. TOOK PHOTOGRAPH OF VEHICLE WITH PLATE LEGIBLE


3. LIMITED TIME WITH SUBJECT


4. KEPT DISTANCE BETWEEN SELF AND SUBJECT


5. DOCUMENTED DESCRIPTION OF SUBJECT AND VEHICLE, DATE AND TIME, LOCATION


6. CONTACTED LAW ENFORCEMENT TO ALERT THEM OF SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY


7. ALERTED OTHER MEMBERS OF COMMUNITY VIA SOCIAL MEDIA





Overall the property owner took more positive actions that both protected her and others' safety.
